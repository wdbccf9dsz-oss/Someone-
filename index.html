A letter for you

I still don't think words will ever be enough to explain everything I have wanted to say to you, but I want to try anyway. You have become someone who means a lot to me, and no matter how complicated things have felt sometimes, that has never been something I could simply pretend away.

Somehow, you became part of my days in ways I didn't expect. The little conversations, the random moments, the laughter, and even the times when we didn't know what to say all became memories I carry with me. It is strange how one person can make ordinary moments feel different.

There are things I wish I had handled better. I know that caring about someone does not automatically mean knowing how to love them perfectly. Sometimes people misunderstand each other, sometimes feelings get too big, and sometimes we struggle to explain what is happening inside us. I know I have things to learn, too.

I never wanted us to become two people who only remember the difficult parts. There were good moments, real feelings, and reasons we cared about each other in the first place. I still appreciate those memories, and I don't want to act as if they meant nothing just because everything has not always been easy.

Little things remind me of you more often than you probably realise. A song, a joke, a certain kind of day, or something I wish I could tell you immediately. Sometimes I catch myself wanting to share something with you before I remember that things are different now.

Loving someone also means understanding that they are their own person. It means listening, being honest, respecting boundaries, and giving each other room to grow. I don't expect everything to be perfect, and I don't want either of us to feel pressured into anything. I just hope that whatever happens, we can be genuine with each other.

Can I be honest? A part of me still cares about you deeply. I cannot promise that I have every answer, and I will not pretend that the past never happened. But I can say that the feelings I had were real, and the place you hold in my memories is real, too.

And if we ever get another chance to understand each other, I would want us to do things with more patience, more communication, and more kindness. Not because we have to recreate what we had before, but because we deserve something that feels safe and honest for both of us.

Really, I made this because I wanted you to have something that shows how much thought I put into you. Every little detail is here for a reason. I hope that when you see it, you understand that you have mattered to me in a way I cannot explain with one simple sentence.

Even if you are unsure what to say, you don't have to find the perfect words. Just be honest with me. Whatever your answer is, I want it to be yours, without pressure or guilt.

Yours, with love. ♡
